# AI-Powered Application Builder — Rubric
**CAI1001C: AI Thinking | 100 Points Total**

Each row below is one rubric criterion. Performance levels: **Excellent** (full points), **Proficient** (~75%), **Developing** (~50%), **Incomplete** (0–25%).

---

## Part 1: Build Your AI Application (50 points)

| # | Criterion | Pts | Excellent | Proficient | Developing | Incomplete |
|---|---|:---:|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **App functionality** | 25 | App runs (live, in preview, or via recorded demo). At least one AI feature works end to end. Problem addressed is real, not a toy demo. Student iterated meaningfully on the AI tool's output. | App runs with minor issues. One feature works but has limitations. Problem is reasonable. Some iteration evident. | App partially runs or key feature broken. Problem is thin or generic. Minimal iteration. | App does not run, or shows no meaningful use of AI capability. |
| 2 | **Use of AI capability** | 15 | App clearly uses a capability we studied (image, NLP, classification, chatbot, etc.). Student can name which chapter introduced it and what the model is doing at a high level. | Capability is present and identifiable but student's understanding of what it does is surface-level. | Capability is used but student cannot connect it to course material. | No identifiable AI capability from course, or purely decorative use. |
| 3 | **Design decisions** | 10 | Student made deliberate design choices about scope, UI, user flow, or feature inclusion. Decisions are visible in the final product. | Most design choices are intentional; one or two feel arbitrary. | App feels like a raw first-pass with little refinement. | No evidence of design thinking. |
