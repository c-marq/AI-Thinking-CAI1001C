Chapter 14 Group Lab: Spot the Concept 10 Points

Robotics Video Analysis — Identifying AI Concepts in Real-World Systems

📋 Instructions

In this activity, your group will watch 2 short video clips of real robotic systems in action. For each video, you'll identify which Chapter 14 concepts you observe and record your observations in the table below.

What to do:

  • Watch each video clip your instructor plays (or the clips linked below)
  • As a group, fill in the observation table — identify what you see for each concept
  • Answer the synthesis question for each video
  • Complete the final group reflection
  • Print to PDF and submit via Canvas

Time: ~30 minutes | Group size: 3–4 students

🎥 Suggested Video Clips (Instructor Reference)

Your instructor will select and play 2 of the following (or similar clips). Each should be 2–4 minutes long:

  • Warehouse Robots: Amazon warehouse fulfillment robots, Ocado automated grocery warehouse
  • Autonomous Vehicles: Waymo self-driving taxi rides, Tesla FSD demonstrations
  • Industrial Robots: Boston Dynamics Spot or Atlas robots, automotive assembly lines
  • Delivery/Service Robots: Starship delivery robots, hospital supply delivery robots
  • Surgical Robots: da Vinci surgical system demonstrations

Group Reflection — Comparing Both Systems

Consider the complexity of sensing, decision-making, navigation, and human interaction. Support your answer with specific observations from the videos.
Think about how the robot learned its behavior. Was it trained on labeled examples? Did it learn through trial and error? Could it have been pre-programmed without any learning at all?
For example: "Adding lidar to the delivery robot (sensor fusion) would help it navigate in low-light conditions."
