TensorFlow Playground (playground.tensorflow.org) is a browser-based tool that lets you build neural networks visually and watch them learn in real time. You'll adjust layers, neurons, learning rate, and other settings — then observe how the decision boundary changes.

Work with your group. Discuss each result before moving to the next task.

Getting Started

  1. Open playground.tensorflow.org in your browser
  2. You'll see a neural network in the center, a dataset on the left, and the output visualization on the right
  3. The play button (▶) starts training. The reset button (↻) clears the model so you can start fresh
  4. The colored regions on the right show the model's decision boundary — how it divides the space into two classes
