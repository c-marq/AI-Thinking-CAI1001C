# CAI1001C: 8-Week Schedule

**Default pacing: two chapters per week.** Parts of the textbook align with weekly groupings, so each week has a coherent theme.

This schedule assumes about three hours of class time plus six to nine hours of student work outside class per week. Sections running on a different cadence (six-week accelerated, sixteen-week traditional) should adapt by compressing or expanding around the same five-part structure.

## Adapting to Other Term Lengths

**16-week term.** One chapter per week. Use the back two weeks for portfolio work and final review. Same five-part structure with breathing room for quiz weeks and discussion deepening.

**6-week accelerated term.** Triple-up the lighter weeks. Suggested compression: combine Weeks 1–2 of this schedule into Week 1, Weeks 3–4 into Week 2, Weeks 5–6 into Week 3, then run Weeks 7 and 8 as standalone Weeks 4 and 5, finishing the portfolio in Week 6. Drop one or two NG assignments and rely on the portfolio as the major summative.
