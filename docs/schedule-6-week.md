# CAI1001C: 6-Week Accelerated Schedule

**Default pacing: two to three chapters per week.** This is a compressed schedule for accelerated sections. Expect roughly twice the weekly workload of the standard 8-week term: about four to five hours of class time plus twelve to fifteen hours of student work outside class.

The five-part textbook structure is preserved, but parts now span weeks rather than aligning to a single week. Week 1 starts gently with Part 1 alone so students have time to set up Colab, GitHub, and Canvas before the technical content ramps up. Week 6 is reserved for the Final Portfolio and capstone synthesis.

## Compression Strategy

This schedule moves fast. To keep the load realistic:

- **Reduce the NG count.** Consider dropping one NG per multi-chapter week. Suggested cuts: NG05 (visualization, since GP05 covers the core skill), NG07 or NG08 (students still get classification practice from the other), NG11 (NLP analysis covered in NG12 chatbot ethics).
- **Combine quizzes.** Quizzes 1 and 2 can run together as a single Week 1 readiness check. Quizzes 6 and 10 can each cover two chapters of context rather than one.
- **Use class time for guided projects.** GP demos in class become the primary instructional vehicle. Chapter readings shift to before-class prep.
- **Lean on the Portfolio.** With less time for individual NG submissions, the Final Portfolio becomes the dominant summative. Weight it more heavily if your section's grading policy allows.

## Notes

- Discussions 2, 4, and 10 carry the bulk of the ethics conversation in this compressed format. Don't skip them.
- The Hands-On Challenge at the end of each chapter is a good substitute when an NG has been dropped. Students still touch the material, instructor still has a deliverable to assess.
- This 6-week schedule reflects the active assessment files in the repo. If your section uses a different cadence or NG set, copy this file and edit the columns.
