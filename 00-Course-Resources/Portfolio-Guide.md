# Final Portfolio Guide
**CAI1001C: AI Thinking | 100 Points**

## Portfolio Components

### Component 1: Three Polished Projects (60 points)

Select 3 of your best projects — one from each section of the course:

- 1 from Chapters 1–5 (Foundations & Python)
- 1 from Chapters 6–9 (Machine Learning & Classification)
- 1 from Chapters 10–11 (Computer Vision & NLP)

For each project, submit:
- A polished Jupyter notebook (cleaned code, clear markdown, working outputs)
- A 1-paragraph reflection (approximately 150 words) on what you learned and how you'd apply it

### Component 2: Competency Reflection Essay (25 points)

A 1-page essay reflecting on your growth across the course's core competencies. Address each of the following and connect your reflection to specific chapters, projects, or Reflect & Discuss prompts from the semester:

1. Examining AI and Machine Learning
2. Applying Ethical Thinking
3. Working with Datasets
4. Building Classification Models
5. Understanding Computer Vision
6. Exploring Natural Language Processing

### Component 3: Recorded Presentation (15 points)

A 5–7 minute recorded presentation showcasing your portfolio. You may record using any of the following:

- PowerPoint with voiceover
- Panopto screen recording
- Canva recording
- Loom, Zoom, or any similar tool

Your presentation should include:
- Brief introduction (who you are, what you learned)
- Project showcase (walk through 2 of your best projects)
- Skills demonstration (what you can now do with AI)
- Future applications (how you'll use this in your field or career)
- Conclusion
