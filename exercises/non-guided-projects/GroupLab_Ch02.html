Activity 2 — Apply the CRAAP Test 15 min
💡 THE CRAAP TEST Chapter 2’s five-criterion check for source quality: Currency, Relevance, Authority, Accuracy, Purpose. Rate each one ✅ (good), ⚠️ (questionable), or ❌ (poor) — and write a one-sentence verdict.
Criterion
Your verdict — one sentence
Rating
Currency
When was it published? Is it still being updated?
Relevance
Does it actually contain the kind of IoT data the title implies? Right level of detail?
Authority
Who collected/published it? Are they credible (university, agency, known researcher)?
Accuracy
Is collection methodology documented? Are there known errors or limitations listed?
Purpose
Why does this dataset exist? Research? Sales? Are there obvious incentives that could bias it?

2.6   Overall verdict: would you trust this dataset for an AI project?
